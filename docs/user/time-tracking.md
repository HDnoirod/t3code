# Time tracking

Open **Time tracking** in the left navigation to record client work. The navigation
can collapse to icons; **Workspace** returns to your coding view.
You can also search for **Open time tracking** in the command palette.

Start with **Clients** to add a name, calendar color, hourly rate and currency
(CAD by default). In the week view, drag on a
day (including weekends) to select time in 15-minute blocks. Enter the client, task,
and optional notes, then save. You can also use **Add time** or click a date header
and enter the start time and number of hours. On touch screens, use these controls
instead of dragging.

Switch between **Week** and **Month**, move through dates, and filter by client.
The totals reflect the selected period and client. Click an entry to edit or delete
it. Drag an existing block to move it: week view changes its day and start time,
while month view changes only its day. Its duration stays the same. Press Escape
to cancel a drag. To move farther, or with a keyboard or touch screen, edit the
entry's date and start time. In month view, click a date to open its week.

Open **Reports** for a Monday–Sunday week, a calendar month, or specific dates.
The start and end dates are both included. Select any combination of clients to
see their hours and amounts. Optionally filter by a hub project, then use **Export CSV**
to download that selection. Assign a project when adding or editing a time entry;
existing entries stay unassigned until you choose one.
Reports include archived clients and separate totals for each currency. Amounts
reflect recorded work, not payments received, and do not include taxes.

Each entry keeps its own hourly rate, so changing a client's rate applies to new
work without changing past amounts. You can correct an individual entry's rate.
Entries created before rates were available are marked **unpriced** and excluded
from earnings. When editing a client, choose **Apply this rate to this client's
existing entries without a rate** to price those entries. This leaves entries
that already have a rate unchanged. A rate of zero means unpaid work; a blank
entry rate means its value is unknown. Amounts are rounded per entry to the nearest cent.

Archive clients you no longer work with; their entries remain visible and can still
be corrected. Restore an archived client before adding new time to it.

Clients and entries are saved in the selected T3 environment and are available from
other web or desktop clients connected to it. If you use multiple environments,
choose one at the top of the calendar. Updates from another device appear on refresh,
when returning to the calendar, or within 30 seconds while it is open. Concurrent
edits are rejected so one device cannot silently overwrite another's work.

Hours are recorded as entered on the chosen calendar date. They do not shift when
you travel or daylight saving time changes. Split work that crosses midnight into
two entries. Receipts are not included yet. The native mobile app does
not have a time-tracking screen; the responsive web calendar works on mobile browsers.
